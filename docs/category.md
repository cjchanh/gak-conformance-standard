# Why GAK is a category, not a badge

A category is real when a third party can test against it and get a verdict.

GAK is authored and maintained by Centennial Defense Systems. Passing a
CDS-provided evaluator is not third-party certification. Every conformance
report published in this repository so far was produced by CDS evaluating its
own kernels against a standard it wrote; treat them as self-assessments.

Two kernels scored to date — Deponent (action-gate) and sworncode
(commit-gate) — prove the harness runs across two gate shapes. They do not
prove independent adoption. Third-party verdicts to date: zero.

The evidence files keep their historical `*-certification.json` names so
existing links resolve; the name is not a certification claim. CDS intends to
publish the first outside implementer's report here, pass or fail; until one
exists there is no independent result to cite.

If you score a kernel against the harness, open an issue with your record.
